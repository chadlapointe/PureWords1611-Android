# Pure Words 1611

**The pure words of the 1611 King James Bible — original text, translator notes, and serious study tools.**

> “The words of the LORD are pure words: as silver tried in a furnace of earth, purified seven times.” — Psalm 12:6

## Vision

Pure Words 1611 is a high-excellence Android study app focused on the pure, unaltered text of the 1611 King James Version. It is designed primarily for serious believers, pastors, and teachers, while also providing a clear path for seekers and those who have been misled by modern versions.

### Core Principles
- Pure 1611 text (including original orthography option)
- Full Apocrypha as published in 1611
- Original 1611 translators’ marginal notes (high priority)
- Deep but flexible explanations (historical, linguistic, and optional light doctrinal)
- Clear, structured seeker path (with choice of full-evidence or gentler version)
- Completely free with optional donations
- Offline-first, privacy-respecting
- Classic + clean modern design

### Primary Platform
Android first (highest quality). Other platforms later.

### Secondary Features (Later)
Word games (Verse Challenge, Word Grid, Word Matching) will be added later as optional “Practice the Pure Words” tools. All original game work is preserved on the `legacy-games` branch.
