## 🎮 What Makes Word Games Successful

### Must-Have Features
✅ Simple, intuitive mechanics (learn in 30 seconds)  
✅ Daily challenges or unlimited play  
✅ Progress tracking (stats, streaks, achievements)  
✅ Social sharing capabilities  
✅ Clean, distraction-free UI  
✅ Fast sessions (1-5 minutes)

### Retention Drivers
- **Habit Formation**: Daily ritual at consistent time
- **Streak Mechanics**: Don't break the chain psychology
- **Progressive Difficulty**: Easy to learn, hard to master
- **Social Competition**: Leaderboards, friend challenges
- **Regular Content**: New puzzles, levels, or features

### Monetization Best Practices
- **Free First**: Build trust and user base before monetizing
- **Optional Ads**: Rewarded only, never forced
- **Fair IAP**: Helpful, not necessary for core experience
- **One-Time Purchase > Subscription**: Less friction, higher satisfaction
- **Transparency**: Clear value proposition for paid features

## ⚠️ What NOT to Do

### Anti-Patterns from Failed Competitors

❌ **Aggressive Monetization** (Words With Friends)
- Ads every 30 seconds during gameplay
- Constant purchase prompts
- Result: User frustration, churn

❌ **Feature Bloat** (Generic Clones)
- 20+ confusing game modes
- Inconsistent UI
- Result: Overwhelmed users abandon

❌ **Ignoring Privacy** (Data-Mining Apps)
- Excessive permissions
- Selling user data
- Result: Distrust, especially from religious users

❌ **Poor Quality Launch** (Rushed Clones)
- Bugs, crashes, typos
- Ugly UI
- Result: 1-2 star reviews, never recover

❌ **No Differentiation** (Generic Wordle Clones)
- Same mechanics, different branding
- Result: Lost in sea of alternatives

✅ Regular updates maintain freshness

### From Words With Friends

⚠️ Dated UI loses to modern competitors
