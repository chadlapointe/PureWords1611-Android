## 🚨 Critical Things You Must Know

### ⚠️ NEVER LOSE YOUR KEYSTORE
- Your app signing keystore is **IRREPLACEABLE**
- If you lose it, you can **NEVER update your app**
- Back it up in **3+ secure locations** immediately
- Store passwords in a secure password manager

### ⚠️ HTTPS is REQUIRED
- Privacy policy **must** be on HTTPS (not HTTP)
- Test your URL in an incognito browser before submitting
- HTTP privacy policies will be **rejected**

### ⚠️ PACKAGE NAME IS PERMANENT
- Once you upload your first AAB, the package name is **locked forever**
- Choose carefully: `com.purewords1611.android`
- You **cannot** change it later

### ⚠️ BE TRUTHFUL
- All policy declarations must be **100% accurate**
- Violations can result in account termination
- When in doubt, be conservative and honest
