## 2. Feature & UI Pattern Breakdown

### A. Feature Sets
*   **Core Gameplay:**
    *   **Daily Challenges:** Almost all top performers feature a daily timed puzzle (e.g., Wordle clones, daily crosswords). This drives retention via streaks.
    *   **Offline Play:** A critical feature for word games. Users expect to play without data. *Competitor gap:* Many subscription-based apps still require online sync, which is a pain point in reviews.
    *   **Difficulty Progression:** Adaptive difficulty (e.g., Wordscapes) or tiered levels (Beginner/Intermediate/Expert) is standard.
*   **Social/Competitive:**
    *   Leaderboards (local or global) are rare in pure word games but common in *Wordscapes*.
    *   Shareable "Result Cards" (e.g., sharing a Wordle grid image) are a key viral marketing tool.
*   **Educational Components:**
    *   *Vocabulary.com* stands out for offering etymology, definitions, and spaced repetition.
    *   Most puzzle-focused apps (Wordscapes, Crossword) offer minimal educational value—they are entertainment, not learning tools. **This is a potential USP for PureWords1611.**

### B. UI Patterns
*   **Minimalist Input:** All top apps use a simple on-screen keyboard or tap-to-select letter grid. No physical keyboard support is required or expected.
*   **Feedback Loops:**
    *   Immediate visual feedback (color changes for correct/incorrect letters).
    *   Haptic feedback (vibration) on button press is standard.
*   **Onboarding:**
    *   Usually 3-5 swipeable screens explaining rules.
    *   "First Puzzle" is always easy to ensure immediate success.
*   **Dark Mode:** Increasingly expected. Most top apps now support it.

### C. Monetization Models
1.  **Freemium + Ads (Most Common):**
    *   Free to download.
    *   Rewarded ads (e.g., "Watch ad to get a hint").
    *   Interstitial ads (between levels/puzzles).
    *   *Pros:* Low barrier to entry, high volume.
    *   *Cons:* Can annoy users; review complaints often cite "too many ads."
2.  **One-Time Purchase (Premium Unlock):**
    *   Common in crossword apps. $2–$5 to remove ads and unlock all puzzles.
    *   *Pros:* Predictable revenue, no ongoing support cost.
    *   *Cons:* Limited lifetime revenue per user.
3.  **Subscription (Less Common in Word Games):**
    *   More common in educational apps (e.g., Vocabulary.com Premium).
    *   $5–$10/month.
    *   *Pros:* Recurring revenue.
    *   *Cons:* High churn rate in casual gaming; users expect word games to be cheap or free.
