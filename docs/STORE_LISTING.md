**Pure Words 1611 - Daily KJV Bible Verses**

Start each day with the pure, unchanging Word of God from the 1611 King James Version Bible. Pure Words 1611 delivers a fresh verse of scripture to your device daily, bringing you closer to God's eternal truth.

**Features:**

📖 **Authentic 1611 KJV Text**  
Experience the Word of God as it was faithfully translated in 1611, preserving the beauty and accuracy of the original King James Version.

📅 **Daily Verse Delivery**  
Receive a new, carefully selected Bible verse each day to inspire, encourage, and strengthen your faith.

🙏 **Simple and Clean Interface**  
Focus on what matters most - God's Word - with a distraction-free, easy-to-use design that puts scripture front and center.

💾 **Offline Access**  
Once downloaded, access your daily verses anytime, anywhere, even without an internet connection.

🔔 **Daily Notifications** (Optional)  
Set a reminder to read your daily verse at a time that works best for you.

📤 **Share Verses**  
Easily share meaningful verses with friends and family through social media, email, or messaging apps.

⭐ **Favorite Verses**  
Save your favorite verses to revisit and meditate on God's promises whenever you need encouragement.

**Why 1611 KJV?**

The King James Version Bible, completed in 1611, has been the gold standard for English Bible translations for over 400 years. Known for its:
- Accurate translation from original Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts
- Beautiful, poetic language that elevates scripture
- Faithful preservation of God's Word across centuries
- Time-tested reliability trusted by millions of believers

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.
