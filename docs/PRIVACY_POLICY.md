## Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

### Internet Access
**Purpose**: To download daily Bible verses (required only on first launch or for updates)  
**Data Transmitted**: Minimal - only verse requests to our content delivery system  
**Can be Disabled**: App works offline after initial verse download

### Notifications
**Purpose**: To send daily reminders for your Bible verse (optional)  
**Data Collected**: None - notifications are generated locally on your device  
**Can be Disabled**: Yes, in App settings or system notification settings

### Storage
**Purpose**: To cache Bible verses for offline reading  
**Data Stored**: Only Bible verse text and your bookmarked verses  
**Location**: App's private storage directory, not accessible to other apps

We do NOT request or use:
- Camera access
- Microphone access
- Location services
- Contacts access
- Phone call information
- SMS or messaging access
